My Research Interests

  • Computer Vision, Image Processing

    Our research involves object detection, domain adaptation, and infrared nighttime surveillance for more accurate and efficient image analysis.

  • Big Data Analytics

    Our lab undertakes industry-academia collaboration projects, equipping students to apply advanced deep learning techniques to big data analysis of real-world datasets.

  • Federated Learning

    Our focus is on combining federated learning with computer vision tasks and designing efficient model aggregation algorithms for collaborative and privacy-preserving machine learning.

  • Blockchain

    We actively develop diverse applications using blockchain technology to enhance security, transparency, and traceability across various parties.
